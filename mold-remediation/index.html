Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. At Ontario Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Ontario homes and commercial properties.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. Throughout the project we maintain negative air pressure containment, run HEPA air scrubbers and follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. Post-remediation verification, including clearance air testing when requested, confirms that your property meets safe occupancy standards.

Why Professional Mold Remediation Matters

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.

Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
  • A vapor barrier is worth considering in a crawl space, which also needs correct ventilation
  • Use mold-resistant drywall and paint in moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and basements
  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen

Warning Signs for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Moisture beading up again and again on glass, walls or anything cold
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Discovering a plumbing or roof leak that has been dripping for a long time
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
